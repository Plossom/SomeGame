# Water Cup · Races 4–8 — plan

Each race has its own world: a new look, new pieces of art and at least one new mechanic. The difficulty climbs
towards the finale, mostly through **narrow channels where you can fall off the sides**.

| # | Race | World | New mechanics | Difficulty |
|---|------|-------|---------------|-----------|
| 4 | Jungle Rapids | a jungle river: green water, temple ruins, a giant waterfall | rapids, crocodiles, temple gates | 3 |
| 5 | Pirate Bay | a pirate cove: a pirate ship, skull rock, wooden docks | cannon fire, treasure coins, plank ramps, the skull cave | 4 |
| 6 | Coral Coast | a reef: bright shallows, coral, rays | reef channels (touch the reef and you run aground), jellyfish, manta rays | 4 |
| 7 | Storm Chasers | a storm out at sea, rocky stacks, a giant whirlpool | cliff-top channels where you fall off the sides, lightning strikes, the whirlpool | 5 |
| 8 | Storm Rally | the storm fortress on its rock | the narrowest channels, waves washing over them, trick leaps between the stacks, the fortress gate | 5 |

## Routes (each race its own shape)
- **4 Jungle Rapids:** a figure of eight; the creek crosses over itself on a sandstone aqueduct.
- **5 Pirate Bay:** a trefoil knot crossing itself three times, over and under in turn.
- **6 Coral Coast:** a maze of long rows and hairpins through the reef.
- **7 Storm Chasers:** round a mountain range, with an alpha loop over itself.
- **8 Storm Rally:** three loops in a chain, crossing itself four times.
- Races 4, 5, 7 and 8 have water caves into mountains: the roof fades while you are inside, the water goes dark and crystals glow.
- Race 4 is a small forest creek with a new forest scenery sheet: oaks, pines, birches and willows (their crowns overhang the creek), ferns, mushrooms, reeds and lily pads.
- Races 4 and 5 are one lap; races 6–8 are two laps.

## New system: ledges (falling off the sides)
- Stretches of the course run along the top of the rocks: beyond the rim there is a sheer drop down to the sea far below.
- Leave the channel there and you fall (the boat shrinks and tumbles away), then you are carried back.
- The rim is drawn as a rocky edge, and the drop as a cliff face falling away into dark water.
- Races 5–8 use more and more of them, and narrower channels (8 → 5).

## Race 4 — Jungle Rapids
- **Look:** a green jungle river, temple ruins and statues on the banks, rope bridges, and a giant waterfall at the
  head of the river (as scenery).
- **Rapids:** stretches with a strong current and boulders to dodge.
- **Crocodiles:** they surface in the channel (bubbles warn first); hit one and you spin.
- **Temple gates:** stone doors slide shut across the river in a rhythm.
- **Jumps:** a log trick jump and gummi bounces.

## Race 5 — Pirate Bay
- **Look:** a pirate cove with a pirate ship at anchor, skull rock, and wooden docks and wrecks.
- **Cannon fire:** the ship fires at the channel. A red ring marks where a cannonball will land; it splashes and
  knocks you aside.
- **Treasure coins:** lines of gold coins along risky lines; each one gives a little boost (TREASURE!).
- **Plank ramps:** wooden ramps that launch you over the docks.
- **The skull cave:** the route runs through skull rock.
- **Rocks and a wreck:** boulders in the cannon zone, a wreck on the home stretch.
- **First ledges:** a short stretch along the cliffs.

## Race 6 — Coral Coast
- **Look:** bright turquoise shallows full of coloured coral, sea turtles and rays.
- **Reef channels:** narrow channels through the reef; touch the coral and you run aground and are set back.
- **Jellyfish:** drifting swarms that spin you.
- **Manta rays:** they leap across the channel (jump through the gap behind them).
- **Jumps:** a coral arch trick jump.

## Race 7 — Storm Chasers
- **Jumps:** two trick leaps from stack to stack.
- **Look:** a stormy sea (dark blue water, rain, a grey-blue gloom) with rocky sea stacks and lighthouses on two of them.
- **Ledges:** channels along the tops of the stacks, where you fall off the sides.
- **Lightning:** a warning ring on the channel ahead, then a bolt strikes there. There's no full-screen flash, just a
  small burst of light where it hits.
- **The giant whirlpool:** it drags you round.

## Race 8 — Storm Rally (finale)
- **Look:** the storm fortress on its rock, a wreck, and the storm at its worst.
- **Ledges:** the narrowest channels of the cup, almost all on ledges.
- **Waves:** they wash across the ledges and shove you towards the edge (warned first).
- **Trick leaps:** two big trick jumps from one rock stack to the next.
- **The fortress gate:** the finish runs through the fortress.
